# Fictional example — River

- Goal: meet local people interested in urban gardening.
- Audience: nearby hobbyists, not commercial clients.

- Topics: small experiments, mistakes, neighborhood observations.

- Capacity: one story each week; no requirement to publish daily.

- Next capture question: what did you try growing this week, and what surprised you?
